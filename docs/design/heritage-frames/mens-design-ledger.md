**No base crest / shield under the nameplate on any theme** — keeps window + plaque airy.

## Theme-specific extra negatives

- **Germany:** no eagle, no iron cross, no black-red-gold flag bars, no “Gott mit uns”
- **England:** no Union Jack, no St George cross as fill, no royal crown on the base shield
- **Italy:** no tricolor flag panels, no Fasces
- **France:** no Tricolore flag fill, no Phrygian-cap revolutionary pile-on
- **Ireland:** no Union Jack, no paramilitary emblems, no leprechaun / cartoon kitsch
- **Mexico:** no national eagle-on-cactus flag device, no sugar-skull holiday takeover, no cartel imagery
- **African-American:** dignified memorial register only — no caricature, no mammy imagery, no chains-as-decoration, no cotton as the sole motif, no confederate symbols, no party-political emblems
