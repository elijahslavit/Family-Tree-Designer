# Pilot financial and capacity model

Status: planning baseline; no spending or purchasing authorization

Owner: founder

Model window: day 1 through month 12

Founder shadow rate: **$40/hour**, fixed for the founding cohort

This model turns the commercial hypotheses into a cash and capacity ledger. Replace every allowance with an invoice or measured result when it exists. Taxes, insurance, legal work, infrastructure, and payment processing must not be purchased or activated without explicit founder approval.

## Control limits

- Maximum gross pilot cash outlay through day 90: **$2,850**.
- Maximum founder-funded net cash gap through day 90: **$2,400**.
- Legal planning reserve inside those limits: **up to $2,500**, contingent on a fixed-fee quote and separate approval.
- Work in progress: **one live real-data project at a time**. A second project may be sold and scheduled, but real materials are not accepted until the first project reaches handoff or the founder records a capacity exception.
- Founder capacity: **10–15 hours/week**. The operating schedule uses 12–15 hours; if only 10 are available, reduce outreach and defer noncritical product work before accepting another delivery.
- Any forecast breach pauses new commitments until the founder explicitly increases the limit, reduces scope, raises price, or stops the experiment.

The limits are risk controls, not authorization to spend up to them.

## Unit assumptions

| Input | Planning value | Treatment |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Founding pilot | $249/project | Exactly two, paid before accepting real materials |
| Immediate price test | $399/project | Begins only after both founding pilots sell |
| Founding renewal | $79/year | Provisional price; confirm the reserve test below before offering renewal |
| Payment fees | 3.5% of cash collected | Conservative allowance until an approved processor quote exists |
| Refund/credit reserve | 2.0% of gross sales | Held as a planning reserve; record actual refunds separately |
| Active variable delivery cash | $18/delivered project | Storage, egress, email/manual delivery, and project-variable tooling allowance |
| Recovery/deletion tail reserve | $6/delivered project | Twelve-month recoverability, notice, export, and deletion allowance |
| Operational contingency | $3.45/delivered project | Conversion, resubmission, or other project-variable exception allowance |
| Shared entry stack | $639/year, about $53.25/month | Across all projects; replace with actual approved invoices |
| Founder labor | $40/hour | Shadow cost, not cash payroll; do not change after seeing cohort results |

The modeled variable cash allowance is **$27.45 per delivered project** before payment fees and refunds. A project fails the cash-contribution test if its collected price does not cover fees, refund reserve, and measured variable cash cost.

Capacity rules:

1. Client delivery and required legal/security work displace new outreach first.
2. At 10 available hours, preserve delivery, interviews already booked, legal gates, and reporting; reduce prospect research/contact volume and record the variance.
3. Do not hide engineering or cleanup time. Classify it as reusable product work or customer-specific delivery.
4. Do not accept real materials for two projects concurrently. A queued client receives a written intake date.
5. If either founding delivery exceeds six founder hours, stop new intake until the overrun is explained and the next scope or price is corrected.

## Renewal floor

The founding $79 renewal is commercially acceptable only if the prior cohort shows:

```text
renewal cash floor = expected active-year variable cash
                   + expected 12-month recovery/deletion tail cash
                   + payment/refund allowance
                   + target cash-contribution reserve
```

Before sending a renewal invoice, calculate that floor from actual storage, egress, notices, support, export, and deletion work. If the floor exceeds $79, honor only an already binding approved promise, then raise or withdraw the renewal for later cohorts with counsel-approved notice. Do not silently subsidize the recovery tail.

## Weekly ledger and decisions

Every Friday record:

- cash collected, invoiced but uncollected, refunds, fees, variable costs, fixed costs, and remaining legal/stack reserves;
- founder hours by sales, legal/security, delivery, support, and reusable engineering;
- project WIP and the next capacity date;
- forecast day-45/day-90 gross outlay and net funding gap; and
- continue, correct, pause, or stop.

The spreadsheet or ledger must implement these equations without hard-coded totals:

```text
cash contribution = collected cash - refunds - payment fees - project-variable cash
net funding gap = cash outflow - collected cash
labor-adjusted result = cash contribution - fixed cash costs - (founder hours × $40)
```

Stop automatically for review when gross outlay is forecast above $2,850, the net gap above $2,400, a second live project would be required, the legal quote is above its reserve, or measured economics make positive labor-adjusted contribution implausible at the tested price.
