# Commercial validation plan

Status: founding-pilot commercial baseline

Owner: founder

Window: day 1 through day 45

All prices and market figures in this document are planning inputs, not public claims. Re-verify third-party prices on official sources before using them in sales material.

## Beachhead

Sell first to solo and two-to-five-person U.S. genealogy practices doing commissioned family-history or legacy work for private clients. A qualified practice:

- already uses Ancestry, Family Tree Maker, RootsMagic, or a comparable workflow and can export GEDCOM;
- typically delivers a narrative PDF, charts, sources, and scanned material;
- completes several paid projects per year;
- serves clients for whom a premium reveal can increase perceived value;
- has an active professional website or association profile;
- is moderately comfortable with software and cares personally about presentation; and
- has an old completed project that could be used after explicit client consent and privacy review.

Exclude forensic, probate, DNA-identification, unknown-parentage, disputed-adoption, citizenship-evidence, and other sensitive case categories during the founding pilots.

## Positioning

> Keep using Ancestry for research and Canva when you need a static page. Family Tree Designer turns the finished work into a branded, private, interactive client experience—tree, stories, photographs, and sources in one beautiful link—without you building a custom website. It makes your final delivery feel more valuable, memorable, and shareable while saving presentation time.

The competitive frame is a premium deliverable and workflow, not research-software replacement.

## Prospect and funnel record

Create one record per prospect with these fields:

- prospect ID, practice, professional name, public profile URL, geography;
- practice size, specialty, typical client/project, estimated projects/year;
- research tools and known GEDCOM capability;
- current deliverables and presentation signals;
- one genuine personalization detail and its source URL;
- qualification score and exclusion reason, if any;
- contact date/channel, founder approval, message version;
- response, booked date, attended date, interview notes;
- pain frequency, priority, current workaround, willingness to change;
- suitable consented project available, legal/privacy fit, urgency;
- quoted price/scope, objection, next step/date, proposal, payment status;
- acquisition hours and any cash acquisition cost;
- outcome reason and permission for future contact.

Do not place client genealogy data or unnecessary personal information in the prospect tracker.

## Interview guide

Keep the workflow interview to 15–25 minutes and spend most of it on past behavior.

1. “Walk me through the last commissioned family-history project you delivered.”
2. “What files or experiences did the client receive, and how did you present them?”
3. “Which part took unpaid or unexpectedly long presentation work?”
4. “What did the client actually open, share, or revisit?”
5. “Have you built a website, slideshow, Canva package, or custom chart? What did it cost in time?”
6. “Show me where GEDCOM, photos, stories, records, and sources live at handoff.”
7. “What privacy, consent, custody, or rights concerns would block a hosted presentation?”
8. Show the two-minute demo. Ask: “What would prevent this from being used on your next suitable project?”
9. “Which already-completed, client-approved project could test it, and when?”
10. State the exact founding scope and price. Ask for a paid next step, not general feedback.

Never request real client data during an interview. Real data can be accepted only after all gates pass and scope/payment/authorization are documented.

## Qualification rubric

Score each item 0, 1, or 2. A project-ready prospect scores at least 12/16 with no automatic exclusion.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Presentation pain | None | Occasional | Recurring, costly, or embarrassing |
| Paid legacy work | None | Adjacent work | Regular commissioned family-history projects |
| Data readiness | Cannot export | Export uncertain | GEDCOM plus organized media/source material |
| Client value | Clients prefer basic files | Mixed | Premium reveal supports fees/referrals |
| Workflow willingness | Will not change | Curious | Will test next suitable project |
| Project readiness | No candidate | Future possibility | Completed client-approved candidate |
| Technology fit | Needs full done-for-you indefinitely | Moderate support | Can complete guided setup under target |
| Privacy fit | Sensitive/excluded case | Needs clarification | Within narrow deceased-person scope |

Automatic exclusions: disallowed case category, no client authorization, unwillingness to attest media rights, expectation of invented/AI family facts, or price below expected variable delivery cost.

The founding-cohort shadow hourly rate is **$40**. Do not change it after seeing results; use a separately labeled sensitivity instead.

## Commercial validation gates

After 10 well-matched interviews, mark the model unvalidated and stop expanding scope if any of these are true:

- fewer than five describe client presentation as recurring real pain;
- fewer than three rank it high enough to change workflow; or
- fewer than two commit a suitable client-approved project and pay a pilot price.

Also pause or pivot when acceptable price stays below variable delivery/hosting cost, professional setup repeatedly exceeds 30–45 minutes, cleanup creates several hours of unscalable founder work with no repeatable remedy, most clients are already satisfied with existing links/PDFs, privacy objections persist after concrete controls, or each buyer requires a unique consulting workflow.

Positive evidence is payment, authorized data, scheduled intake, completed delivery, client use, and repeat purchase intent—not praise.
